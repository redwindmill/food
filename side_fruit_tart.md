## FRUIT TART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**INGREDIENTS**
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 3 tbsp corn starch
- 4 large egg yolks
- 2 cups milk
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tsp vanilla

**COOK**
- add sugar cornstarch in a large pan
- add egg yolks; mix until light and thick
- whisk in milk slowly until very smooth
- cook over medium low heat until thick
- add butter and vanilla, mix well
- put in bowl, cover with plastic wrap (press into cream
- cool completely (overnight works)

**CRUST**

**INGREDIENTS**
- 3 cups bread flour
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup butter
- 2 large egg
- 2 tbsp whole milk
- 2 tsp vanilla

**PREP**
- using a mixer or blender: blend flour, sugar and salt
- add in butter; work with pastry knife or blend until butter is nothing but small chunks
- add egg and vanilla; blend until clumpy
- pat dough into 2 chunks, wrap in plastic wrap, set into fridge (2 hours or overnight)

**BAKE**
- roll dough and place into pie tins
- use fork to poke dough in bottom of tin
- bake in bottom of the rack at 350 until golden (15-18 min)
- let cool completely before filling

**FILL AND TOP**
- fill pie crusts with cream
- top with seasonal fruit.

Fruit combos:
- strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, blackberries
- tangerines, mandarins, clementines, kumquats
- pineapple, mango, kiwi
