### (Air) Fried Pork Bao

### Ingredients

### Dough

* 2 3/4 cups bread flour
* 1 cup warm water
* 1 tbsp sugar
* 1 1/2 tsp active dry yeast
* 1/2 tsp baking powder
* 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

### Filling

* 1 lb ground pork shoulder
* 1 tsp lawry's Seasoned Salt
* 2 tbsp Corn Starch
* 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
* 1/16 tsp (generous pinch) Five-Spice Powder
* 3 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
* 3/4 cups ginger Water (3/4 cup water infused with 2 tsp sliced ginger)
* 1 1/2 cups Greens (50/50 mix of chopped scallions and finely diced white onion)

### Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Dough

1. In a mixing bowl, dissolve the sugar and active dry yeast in the warm water. Let sit for 1 minute.
2. Add the flour, baking powder, and vegetable oil to the bowl.
3. Mix and knead until a smooth dough forms and pulls away cleanly from your hands and the bowl.
4. Cover with a damp towel and let rest for 15 minutes.

### 2. Mix the Filling

1. Slice the ginger and place it in 3/4 cup of water to create the ginger water. Set aside for 15 min
2. In a large bowl, combine the ground pork, Lawry's salt, corn starch, black pepper, and five-spice powder.
3. Pour in the soy sauce and toasted sesame oil. Mix vigorously in one direction until the meat becomes sticky and paste-like.
4. Strain the ginger pieces out of the water. Pour the ginger water into the meat 1/4 cup at a time, beating vigorously after each addition until the liquid is fully absorbed.
5. Gently fold in the scallions and diced white onions. Place the filling in the refrigerator to chill.

### 3. Assemble and Shape

1. Cut the rested dough ball into 16 equal portions (roughly 45g each).
2. Flatten a dough portion with your palm, then use a rolling pin to roll from the edge toward the center.
3. Rotate the dough piece and repeat, leaving a thick center "belly" and thin edges.
4. Place a scoop of chilled filling in the center, pleat the edges upward, and pinch the top shut to seal.

### 4. Cook

### Option A: Stovetop Steam

1. Set up a stovetop steamer using a plate balanced on aluminum foil balls inside a large pot with a lid. Fill with 1 inch of water.
2. Wrap a clean kitchen towel around the inside of the pot lid to absorb condensation.
3. Line the plate with punched parchment paper and arrange the buns with space to expand.
4. Cover and steam on medium heat for 15 minutes.
5. Turn off the heat and leave the lid closed for 5 minutes before opening.

### Option B: Air Fryer (Crispy Bottoms)

1. After completing the 15-minute stovetop steam, transfer the buns to an air fryer basket.
2. Air fry for roughly 15 minutes to crisp up the exterior.GGgg
